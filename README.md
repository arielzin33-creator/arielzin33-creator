## 🙋 About Me

I'm a versatile developer specialising in **the MERN stack and AI-agent
development**, with a passion for continuous learning. My expertise lies in
integration, improving user interfaces, and enhancing overall user
experiences — plus practical experience directing AI-assisted development
tools (Base44, Claude Code) against clear specifications, rather than
accepting first-pass output.

- 🎓 &nbsp;Completing an intensive **Full-Stack &amp; AI Development Bootcamp** — capstone in progress
- 🔭 &nbsp;Recently shipped **CirqHub**, an AI-driven circular-economy platform, at a hackathon
- 🌱 &nbsp;Learning **LangChain, LangGraph and MCP** — agentic AI, not just prompting
- 🧪 &nbsp;Before code: 10+ years leading R&amp;D teams in materials science and medical devices
- 📫 &nbsp;Reach me at **arielzin33@gmail.com**
